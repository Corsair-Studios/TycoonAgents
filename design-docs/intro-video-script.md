# TycoonAgents Intro Video Script

Target length: 2–3 minutes. No absolute paths shown on screen.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)

**On screen:** Real footage of the user's own team working — the stage as tasks complete today and objects start building.

**Voiceover:** Watch your AI team build while it works.

## Scene 2 — Install & First Run (0:10–0:25)

**On screen:** Installing the extension, opening the panel for the first time, the first-run setup steps.

**Voiceover:** Install it once, and it sets itself up alongside the tools you already use.

## Scene 3 — The Stage & the Tycoon Loop (0:25–0:55)

**On screen:** Completed tasks filling the stage with built objects, a rival appearing while agents are active, the team repairing a damaged object, then a rebirth on the badge HUD resetting the stage.

**Voiceover:**
- **This is the loop:** your team's finished work builds the stage.
- **Stay active, and rivals show up** — your team fights them off and repairs what gets hit.
- **Rebirth resets the stage for permanent perks** — and some surprises unlock as you rebirth.

## Scene 4 — Delegation & Cost Savings (0:55–1:10)

**On screen:** The Metrics panel's Delegation view, showing the orchestrator handing work to specialist agents.

**Voiceover:** Behind the scenes, one orchestrator delegates to specialists — and Metrics shows you exactly what that's saving.

## Scene 5 — The Routing Check (1:10–1:25)

**On screen:** The routing check screen: a token-free pass/fail table, then the optional live check labeled with its real cost and scope.

**Voiceover:** A quick check confirms your keywords route to the right specialist — no guessing, and no surprise charges. **(once the tour ships)**

## Scene 6 — Harness & Other AIs (1:25–1:45)

**On screen:** Choosing a harness (Claude Code as default), then adding another AI provider with fallback shown in the Agents view.

**Voiceover:** Pick which tool runs your agents, and add other AIs as backup — TycoonAgents falls back automatically if one's unavailable.

## Scene 7 — Project Plan & Approval Gates (1:45–2:00)

**On screen:** A registered plan with phases and tasks, a human gate awaiting approval.

**Voiceover:** For bigger work, TycoonAgents can break it into a plan with phases — and pause for your approval at the checkpoints that matter.

## Scene 8 — Themes (2:00–2:20)

**On screen:** Switching a built-in theme, importing a theme .zip, opening the Theme Builder, and the animation preview during theme review.

**Voiceover:**
- **Switch themes instantly, or import your own.**
- **The Theme Builder can generate one with AI, or you can upload your own art for free** — and preview it before you keep it.

## Scene 9 — Handbook & Help (2:20–2:35)

**On screen:** Opening the handbook, running Troubleshoot & Fix, and the feedback option.

**Voiceover:** Stuck? The handbook, a built-in troubleshooter, and quick feedback are always one click away.

## Scene 10 — Outro & Call to Action (2:35–2:50)

**On screen:** The stage in full swing, then the product name and install link.

**Voiceover:** Your AI team is already working. Watch it build.

**Total runtime: approximately 2:50.**
