Building a Theme

The form
Before you generate anything, the Theme Builder form asks for every piece that makes up a theme. This chapter is the field-by-field contract — what each slot is, how many you need, and what makes a good description.
§ 5.1 · Name and creative direction
the seed everything else follows

Start with a theme name and a creative direction textarea — a short paragraph describing the overall look and feel (for example, a neon-city theme, or a high-seas theme). This is the seed that every subsequent image prompt draws from, so it's worth spending a minute getting it specific. An environment description follows, covering the background/stage setting your characters will be built into.

§ 5.2 · Characters, rivals, objects, decor
the roster
Slot Count Notes
Characters 16 Per-slot name + description each.
Rivals 4 Each has an attack style — melee or ranged — plus a description.
Objects 21 across 2 boards 19 static + 2 animated. Includes a crate, poof FX, and a projectile description.
Decor 9 Background/set-dressing pieces.
§ 5.3 · Expressions
fixed labels, themed art

Every theme has exactly 8 expressions, and the labels are fixed — you don't get to add or remove them, only re-skin their art to match your theme:

Love · Idea spark · Anger · Team rally · Thumbs up · Music · Sleep/zzz · Dazed

§ 5.4 · Badges
up to 25

Up to 25 achievement badges round out the form. Badge names are editable, and their art is generated alongside everything else in the pipeline (Chapter 06).

§ 5.5 · Writing good descriptions
two habits that pay off
Tip

Concrete visual descriptions beat vague ones. "A weathered dock worker in a faded blue coat with a hook hand" generates far more consistent art than "a worker" — every image prompt in the pipeline consumes the exact per-item description you write here, so specificity up front saves you retries later.

Tip

Describe characters in shoulder-height poses rather than full-body extremes. Wide poses (arms fully outstretched, deep lunges) can clip at the edges of the character board when it's sliced into frames. Chapter 07 covers the details and how to recover if it happens anyway.
