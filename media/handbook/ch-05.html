05

Theme Service: Sign-in & Credits

Before you build
AI theme generation runs through the Corsair-hosted Theme Service and spends credits you buy in packs — there's no API key to find or paste in. Uploading or hand-making your own art (Import .zip) stays completely free and needs no account at all.
§ 5.1 · What the Theme Service does
authoring-time only

The Theme Service plans and generates a theme's art on Corsair's infrastructure — TycoonAgents sends it your theme description, creative direction, and per-item fields, and it returns the assembled prompt plus the generated images. This only happens while you're actively generating. Once images come back, they're saved locally as part of the theme (Chapter 07 covers exactly what gets stored), and the extension never calls the service again to render the scene at runtime — it just plays back the stored frames.

§ 5.2 · Signing in
magic link or Google

Opening the Theme Builder for the first time asks you to sign in — by an emailed magic link, or with Google. In VS Code (and forks like Insiders, VSCodium, Cursor, Windsurf, or Antigravity), the Google callback deep-links back into whichever editor you started sign-in from; if it ever lands somewhere with no sign-in pending, you'll see the code in a notification with a Copy code button to paste into the Theme Builder instead. In the standalone host, Google completes through a local browser callback, with a paste-the-code fallback if that callback can't reach your browser session.

Note

Your account ID is shown in the Theme Builder with a copy button next to it. Include it whenever you contact support about a store or credit issue — it's the fastest way for the maintainer to look up your account.

§ 5.3 · Credits & board costs
what generation spends

Every generation attempt spends credits, including retries, and the plan review screen shows the cost before each attempt so you're never guessing. Attempts cost 2 credits for object, expression, and achievement boards, and 3 credits for character, rival, and environment boards.

Tip

Re-slicing an existing image is always free, no matter how many times you do it — only a fresh generation attempt spends credits.

Note

Failed generations caused by provider or network errors, and generations the provider refuses on content-policy grounds, are refunded automatically. Prompts blocked by content screening or a locked account are never charged at all. Credits are not refunded for images you generated successfully but simply don't like — spend a retry instead.

§ 5.4 · Buying credits
Stripe Checkout, three packs
Pack Credits Price Bonus badge
Starter 45 $4.99 — (no bonus)
Theme 100 $9.99 "+11% credits"
Bundle 205 $19.99 "+14% credits"

The store shows each pack's total credit count and price as above. The "Best value" chip and the bonus badge on the Theme and Bundle packs are computed from a base credit count baked into the pack — the badge percentage is bonus credits divided by that base, rounded to the nearest whole percent (for example, the Theme pack's badge comes from 10 bonus credits over a 90-credit base, and the Bundle pack's from 25 over 180). The Starter pack carries no bonus and shows no badge.

Buy credits opens Stripe Checkout in your browser; your balance updates when you return to the panel. A typical 23-board theme runs about 95 credits, a careful run about 55, and heavy retries up to about 170 — output quality tracks prompt quality, so a clear, specific description usually costs less than a vague one you keep retrying (see § 6.5 · Writing good descriptions).

§ 5.5 · Acceptable use
strikes & locked accounts

Prompts and reference images are screened automatically before generation runs (see § 8.1). A generation the model refuses on content-policy grounds is refunded, but still counts as a strike; three strikes within 24 hours locks the account, disabling both generation and purchases. A locked account can request an unlock through Submit Feedback (§ 14.6).

§ 5.6 · Advanced: pointing at a different endpoint
testing only

The advanced setting tycoonagents.themeService.endpoint (VS Code) or the THEME_SERVICE_ENDPOINT environment variable (standalone) points the client at a different https Theme Service URL for testing, and is normally left empty. Sign-in settings (the Supabase project and public key) are fetched from whichever Theme Service you're pointed at, so this also switches the sign-in project.

Note

An older, key-based image path (per-provider OpenAI/Gemini API keys, model overrides, and image quality) was removed from Settings in 0.0.670. All generation runs through the Theme Service described in this chapter.
