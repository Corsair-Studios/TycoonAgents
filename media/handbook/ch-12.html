12UEFN Power Tools

For Fortnite Creative projects

If your workspace is a UEFN (Unreal Editor for Fortnite) project, TycoonAgents adds a second layer on top of everything else: a bridge into the editor itself, so agents can inspect and audit your level, not just your source files.

§ 12.1 · Setup detected automatically

The setup wizard detects a UEFN project on its own. When it does, it offers to:

01

Stage the Python bridge into your project's Content/Python folder.

02

Install the Verse Expert and UEFN Auditor agents alongside your other specialists.

Screenshot

Setup screen — UEFN detected, bridge staging chip.

See also

The UEFN chip in the stage's status chip row (Chapter 03, § 3.6) shows live bridge connectivity at a glance: green glow means a heartbeat arrived in the last 15 seconds and agents can use the uefn_* MCP tools; amber means reopen the project in UEFN so the bridge auto-starts.

§ 12.2 · The Power Tools window in-editor launcher

Once the bridge is staged, an in-editor launcher window becomes available inside UEFN itself. Its title bar shows the bridge's version, so you can tell at a glance whether it's current. From there you can reach:

ToolWhat it does
Device auditReviews devices in your level for common configuration issues.
Project health scanChecks the project for structural issues across its assets and Verse source.
Asset browsersBrowse and inspect assets in your project's content.

§ 12.4 · Keeping the bridge current self-updating, mostly

The bridge keeps itself up to date in two ways: the newest project-side copy wins automatically when a UEFN project loads, and the extension also refreshes engine-side copies each time the panel opens.

Tip

If the Power Tools window's title shows an older version than your installed extension, close and reopen the UEFN project — the self-sync runs at project load, before anything else.

§ 12.5 · Revision control hygiene .urcignore

Setup also writes a .urcignore file so day-to-day dev artifacts (config, logs, and similar loose files) stay out of UEFN's Revision Control — only your actual project assets get tracked.
